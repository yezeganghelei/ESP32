# ESP32 MicroPython Introductory Tutorials

Welcome to Waveshare ESP32 MicroPython Introductory Tutorials!

This series of Tutorials is based on the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Zero Mini Development Board and the Thonny IDE environment, designed to help learners get started with ESP32 development. It is recommended to study in order, but you can also choose chapters of interest based on your own needs.

Important Note: About Board Compatibility

The core logic of this tutorial applies to all ESP32 development boards, but all hardware operation example codes are based on  . To ensure consistency in the learning experience, it is recommended to use a Development Board based on the ESP32-S3 chip.
If you use other ESP32 development boards, please note:

- **Different pin definitions**: different Development Boards will have different pin numbering and functional layouts.
- **Different onboard resources**: for example, your Development Board may use LEDs on different pins, or may not have an onboard RGB LED.

**Before running any examples that interact with hardware, please check your board's pinout diagram and modify the pin numbers in the code to ensure proper operation.**

You can use any ESP32 development board you have, along with your own components to follow along.
