# Photoresistor



<img src="img/photoresistor.png" class="align-center" width="200" alt="image" />



A photoresistor or photocell is a light-controlled variable resistor. The resistance of a photoresistor decreases with increasing incident light intensity; in other words, it exhibits photo conductivity.



A photoresistor can be applied in light-sensitive detector circuits and light-activated and dark-activated switching circuits acting as a resistance semiconductor. In the dark, a photoresistor can have a resistance as high as several megaohms (M惟), while in the light, a photoresistor can have a resistance as low as a few hundred ohms.
