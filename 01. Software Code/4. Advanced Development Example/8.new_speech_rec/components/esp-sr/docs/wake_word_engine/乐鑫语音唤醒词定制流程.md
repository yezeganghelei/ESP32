#### two、Training corpus requirements

Customers can prepare their own training corpus or purchase it from a third party. The corpus has the following requirements:

- 语料音频Format要求
	- Sampling rate: 16 KHz
	- Encoding: 16-bit signed int
	- Number of channels (channel): mono
	- Format：wav	

- Corpus collection requirements
	- Number of people sampled：It is best that the sample can be larger than 500 people，Among them men and women，Balanced age distribution，Children not less than 100 people 
	- Sampling environment：Low ambient noise（< 40 dB），建议在语音室wait专业环境下Record
	- Record场景：distance microphone 1 m 处每peopleRecord 15 all over，in 5 speaking rapidly，5 normal speech rate，5 speaking slowly；distance microphone 3 m 处每peopleRecord 15 all over，in 5 speaking rapidly，5 normal speech rate，5 speaking slowly
	- Record设备：High fidelity microphone
	- Sample naming must reflect sample information：like female_age_fast_id.wav Or have a separate table to record the age of each sample，性别wait信息
	
#### three、硬件设计与test

The voice wake-up effect is closely related to the hardware design and cavity structure. To ensure the reasonable design of the hardware equipment, please read the following content carefully.

- Hardware design requirements     
	- For various types of voice speaker designs，Espressif可提供 Schematic diagram／PCB etc. design reference，Customers can design and modify it according to their specific needs，After the design is completed，Espressif可提供ReviewServe，Avoid common design problems。

	- cavity structure，最好有专门的声学people员参与设计，Espressif不提供 ID Design reference，Customers can use mainstream speaker designs on the market as a reference
		> For example Tmall Elf、Xiaodu speakers、谷歌音箱wait
